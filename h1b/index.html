H1b · Proof of real + age (arm B of H1)

Everything in H1a plus the age, before paying: “•••• 36” on every blurred card, the age in the preview sheet and in the push, and the age range on the paywall (“Aged 26–36”). H1a and H1b run against the same control, so the effect of age is measured on its own.

Hypothesis

Problem. A free user can neither check a reaction nor answer it, so interest is lost and Gold looks like a gamble. Arm B of H1: the same “check”, plus the age.

If on top of H1a every blurred reaction shows the sender’s age next to the hidden name, the push names the age, and the paywall shows the age range,

then paywall-to-purchase and Gold per opener will be higher than in H1a, and 7-day cancellations and refunds lower,

because age is one of the first filters in dating, and many reactions come from outside the age range the user wants; today they learn it only after paying.

Test: three arms — control, H1a, H1b; about 50% more openers than a two-arm test. Guardrails as in H1a; the city stays hidden until Gold.
