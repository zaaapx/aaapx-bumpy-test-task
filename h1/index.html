Hypothesis

If we show on every blurred card the sender’s country, a Like tag when the reaction is a Like, and “Active today” only when it is true, and open the paywall with real counts,

then the share of Liked You openers who tap a card or “See who liked you” will increase, and so will Gold purchases per opener, with no rise in fake-profile reports,

because today a like can’t be checked before paying (“I think they are bots to make me pay”), users ask for the country, and partial information makes people more curious than none.

Primary metric: share of openers who tap a card or the CTA (baseline 3.1%). Guardrails: fake-profile reports, revenue per opener.
